# Changelog

## [2.1] - 2026-11-15
- New interface drawn like a physical remote: one screen, no scrolling, vector icons, app keys in Netflix/YouTube/Prime Video/Disney+ style, IR LED indicator
- Big D-pad that also works as a touchpad; VOL and CH rockers; number pad on demand
- Voice commands in Italian through the phone's speech service, offline when available
- Brand chip, model picker and model finder moved to the ⋯ menu
- 6 new unit tests for voice commands (16 total)

## [2.0] - 2026-11-15
- Database of 107 brands and 348 remotes generated from Flipper-IRDB
- Encoders for NEC, NECext, NEC42, Samsung32, Sony SIRC 12/15/20, RC5/RC5X, RC6 and raw codes
- App shortcuts (YouTube, Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Spotify, Apps), playback and colour keys
- Touchpad area: drag for arrows, tap for OK
- Brand search, model chips, automatic model finder per brand and across all brands
- 10 unit tests
